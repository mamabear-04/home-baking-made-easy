# Brand Direction: The Local Crumb

## Visual Essence
The Local Crumb is a hyper-local, artisanal micro-bakery. The visual identity should feel **warm, craft, and neighborhood-focused**, blending **rustic textures** with a **modern, minimalist layout**. It is the bridge between the tradition of sourdough baking and the convenience of a modern digital pre-order platform.

---

## Typography Direction
*   **Primary Typeface (Headers):** A modern, elegant serif with slightly rounded terminals. It should feel established but not "stiff." (Example: *Lora*, *Fraunces*)
*   **Secondary Typeface (Body/UI):** A clean, high-legibility geometric sans-serif. (Example: *Inter*, *Montserrat*)
*   **Brand Mark Style:** Minimalist, hand-stamped aesthetic. Clean lines, organic shapes (wheat stalks, bread scoring patterns).
